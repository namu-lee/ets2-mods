Description
Adds a speed limit sign on the top left corner to your dashboard GPS, with
your current speed displayed directly below it.

- Size and placement are adjusted to fit each GPS screen and avoid
  overlapping its controls.
- Supports left- and right-hand-drive interiors on SCS trucks and Coaches
  DLC vehicles equipped with a factory GPS.

Compatibility
- Works with ETS2 1.62 and above.
- May conflict with mods that change the dashboard or cluster. Any mod with
  higher priority wins.
