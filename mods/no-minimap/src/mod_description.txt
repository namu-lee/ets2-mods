No Minimap
Source code: https://github.com/namu-lee/ets2-no-minimap

Description
Hides the HUD minimap on desktop and in VR. Keep the Navigation widget enabled to see the clock, rest indicators, and route ETA.

- The clock, fatigue gauge, and mandatory break timer share one row. The layout adjusts to the enabled indicators.
- The ETA appears when a destination is set. When the route is cleared, the ETA row folds away and the widgets above it move down.

Compatibility
- Tested on ETS2 1.62.3.3.
- May conflict with mods that modify the Route Advisor. If two mods change the same layout, the higher-priority mod takes precedence.
